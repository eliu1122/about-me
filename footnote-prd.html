Footnote

Grounded Q&A over SEC filings: ask a plain-English question about a US public company and get an answer built only from what it has filed.

1. Problem

People researching a company use chatbots to get through its 10-K faster, but they can’t tell whether a conclusion came only from the filing or from somewhere else. So they re-check each answer against the source themselves, which gives back most of the time the chatbot saved.

Evidence: interviews with 6 target users about how they research 10-K filings. The re-checking came up as the common workaround.

2. Target users

People who read a US public company’s filings to understand the business and its risks before making a decision, and who need to be able to defend where a claim came from.

3. Goal

A user can ask a question about a company and trust the answer without opening the filing to verify it.

4. Scope

v1v2 (later)
One company per questionCompare companies in one answer
Any US company with a tickerForeign issuers and funds
Latest 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, DEF 14AAlerts on new filings

Out of scope for both: investment advice, and any source other than the company’s own filings.

5. Requirements (v1)

  1. The user types a ticker; its recent filings are downloaded and indexed while a progress bar shows.
  2. The user asks a question in plain English and gets an answer scoped to that one company.
  3. The answer is written only from passages retrieved from the filings.
  4. Every quote is checked against the source before it is shown, and links back to the filing section it came from.
  5. Financial tables are read as tables, so a question about a line item is answered from the table’s values.
  6. If the filings don’t cover the question, the product says “not disclosed” instead of guessing.

6. Success metrics

All four are scored on a reviewed evaluation set built from real filings, and tracked against a saved baseline so a change that makes things worse is visible.

MetricWhat it asksBar
Answer accuracy Is the answer right, judged against the filing? Improves on the baseline
Citation accuracy Does the cited passage exist and support the answer? 100%
Abstention Does it decline the questions the filings can’t answer? Improves on the baseline
Outside content Does anything in the answer come from outside the filings? Zero

7. Risks and open questions

  • A wrong answer with a confident citation is worse than no answer, so declining has to be reliable, not just possible.
  • Thresholds for when to decline may not carry over from the company they were tuned on to others.
  • Checking that a quote is genuine does not prove it supports the claim built on it.
  • Table-heavy questions are the hardest to get right and need their own handling.
